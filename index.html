ABOUT VOICEGUARD

Protecting people from synthetic voices.

VoiceGuard is a prototype for detecting AI-generated and manipulated speech before it can be used to deceive someone.

Our Mission

Make voice-based fraud harder by giving users a clear, understandable risk assessment instead of forcing them to judge a suspicious call themselves.

How It Works

Audio is preprocessed, analyzed for synthetic speech characteristics, and converted into a confidence and risk assessment.

Known Limitations

No detector is perfect. New voice-cloning systems, compression, background noise, and short recordings can reduce detection accuracy.

Privacy

For a production version, audio should be processed securely, retained only when necessary, and protected with explicit user consent.
